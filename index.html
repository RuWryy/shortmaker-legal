Shortmaker

A personal tool for preparing and scheduling short vertical videos.

Shortmaker is a desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. It cuts long videos into short clips, adds subtitles and a background video, and schedules publication to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

It is used by one person, its developer, with that person's own accounts. It is not a public service and has no user registration.
