What Shortmaker is

Shortmaker is a personal software tool that runs locally on the developer's computer. It prepares short video clips and, through the official TikTok and YouTube APIs, sends them to the developer's own accounts after the account owner authorizes the connection.

Who may use it

Shortmaker is intended for use by its developer with their own accounts only. It is not offered to the public and does not publish content for third parties.

Content

Only content that the account owner has the right to publish is used, either their own videos or videos whose license allows reuse (for example Creative Commons with attribution). Required attribution is kept with the clip. Each TikTok post is reviewed and confirmed by the account owner, who chooses the privacy setting before it is sent.

Platform rules

Use of TikTok and YouTube through Shortmaker is subject to the terms and policies of those platforms. The developer is responsible for the content published from their accounts.

No warranty

The software is provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
