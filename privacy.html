Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Summary

Shortmaker runs only on the developer's own computer. It has no server and no database operated by the developer, and it does not collect data about other people.

What it accesses

After the account owner signs in with TikTok, Shortmaker receives an access token and, with the permissions granted, the account's basic profile information (user.info.basic), and can upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish) and list the account's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list).

How it is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how those videos perform. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored in local files on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer and are not sold or shared with third parties. Video files and subtitles are processed locally. Text from video transcripts may be sent to an AI service to choose clip boundaries; no TikTok account data is included.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time in Shortmaker's settings or in TikTok's app settings, and can delete the local token file. Disconnecting stops all access.
